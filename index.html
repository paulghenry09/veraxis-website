The EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001, and the Kenya Data Protection Act all require AI developers to demonstrate clean, traceable training data. Every one of them relies on narrative self-certification. There is no standardised technical artifact a competent authority can independently verify.

V-TSAL closes this gap. A Training Signal Receipt is a cryptographically signed record — verifiable by anyone, anywhere, using a single published public key. No account. No registration. No contact with Veraxis required.

This is the Certificate Authority model applied to AI governance. Veraxis is to AI training data what DigiCert is to HTTPS — the root of trust the entire ecosystem routes through.

The Protocol

Five phases. One immutable chain.

01
Source Connect
Read-only connection to your data source via IAM role assumption. Nothing is copied. Nothing leaves your infrastructure.
02
Merkle Build
Every file hashed in BLAKE3. Combined into a complete Merkle tree. One changed byte invalidates the root.
03
Forensic Scan
34 signal detectors in parallel. PII, copyright, authenticity, geo-fraud, bias. 99.99994% verification guarantee.
04
HSM Sign
BLAKE3 root submitted to the Hardware Security Module. Ed25519 private key signs it. Key never leaves the HSM.
05
TSR Issued
Signed Training Signal Receipt delivered to you and logged to the public transparency ledger simultaneously.
